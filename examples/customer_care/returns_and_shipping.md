# Northwind Outfitters — Returns, Shipping & Warranty Policy

*Effective January 1, 2026. Supersedes all prior versions.*

## Returns

Customers may return most items within **45 days** of the delivery date for a full
refund to the original payment method. The item must be unworn, unwashed, and in
its original packaging with tags attached.

**Final sale items** — clearance merchandise, gift cards, and personalized
engraved products — cannot be returned or exchanged under any circumstances.

**Worn footwear** may be returned within 45 days only if the customer is enrolled
in the Trail Guarantee program (free, enrollment at time of purchase).

### How to start a return

1. Sign in and open **Orders → Return an item**.
2. Select the item and the reason for return.
3. Print the prepaid return label that is generated.
4. Drop the package at any carrier location within 14 days of generating the label.

Return labels expire 14 days after generation. An expired label must be
regenerated from the same screen; there is no charge for regeneration.

### Refund timing

Refunds are issued within **3 business days** of the returned item arriving at
the warehouse. The customer's bank may take an additional 5–10 business days to
post the credit. Original shipping charges are refunded only when the return is
the result of our error (wrong item shipped, item defective on arrival).

### Return shipping cost

| Reason for return | Who pays return shipping |
|---|---|
| Defective or damaged on arrival | Northwind |
| Wrong item shipped | Northwind |
| Changed mind / wrong size ordered | Customer — $6.95 deducted from refund |
| Trail Guarantee footwear claim | Northwind |

## Exchanges

Direct exchanges are available for size and color changes on the same product.
Exchanges ship as soon as the return is scanned by the carrier — the customer
does not wait for the warehouse to receive it. If the returned item never
arrives, the customer is charged for the exchange item after 30 days.

## Shipping

| Service | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 5–7 business days | Free on orders over $75, otherwise $5.95 |
| Expedited | 2–3 business days | $14.95 |
| Overnight | Next business day | $29.95 |

Orders placed after **2:00 PM Central Time** are processed the following business
day. Overnight orders must be placed before 12:00 PM Central Time to ship the
same day.

We ship to all 50 US states. We do not currently ship internationally or to
PO boxes for Expedited and Overnight services.

### Order changes and cancellation

Orders can be modified or cancelled within **60 minutes** of being placed, via
**Orders → Cancel order**. After 60 minutes the order enters the fulfillment
queue and cannot be changed; the customer must wait for delivery and return it.

### Lost or delayed packages

A package is considered lost if tracking has not updated for **7 consecutive
days**. Customers should contact support, who will file a carrier claim and ship
a replacement at no cost. Claims must be filed within 30 days of the ship date.

## Warranty

All Northwind-branded gear carries a **2-year limited warranty** against defects
in materials and workmanship, starting from the delivery date.

The warranty covers: seam failure, zipper and hardware failure, delamination,
and water-resistance failure in items rated as water resistant.

The warranty does **not** cover: normal wear and abrasion, damage from improper
care, animal damage, accidental rips and punctures, or loss and theft.

Third-party brands sold by Northwind are covered by their own manufacturer
warranties; Northwind facilitates the claim but does not administer it.

### Filing a warranty claim

Customers submit photographs of the defect through **Support → Warranty claim**.
Claims are reviewed within **5 business days**. Approved claims result in repair,
replacement, or store credit at Northwind's discretion. Proof of purchase is
required; a registered account order history satisfies this requirement.

## Account and payment

Customers can update payment methods under **Account → Wallet**. We accept Visa,
Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, and Northwind gift cards.

Gift cards never expire and cannot be redeemed for cash except where required by
state law.

For account access issues, customers should use the password reset link. Support
agents cannot change an account email address without identity verification.

## Escalation

If a customer is dissatisfied with a resolution, or the issue involves an order
over $500, escalate to a Tier 2 specialist at **support-escalation@northwind.example**
with the order number in the subject line. Tier 2 responds within 1 business day.
